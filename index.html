Le Nhat Kha (Kai)

AI Research Engineer

Agntic.ai · Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam

I build AI systems at Agntic.ai, working on agents and LLM-powered tooling. Before this I spent close to two years at MoMo, Vietnam's largest super app, putting computer vision and multimodal models into products used by tens of millions of people, and a stretch at VinUniversity's Center for AI Research on reinforcement learning for traffic control.

One question runs through most of what I've worked on: what happens when a model meets data it was never trained for. My WACV paper closes the gap between synthetic and real scene text by crossing it in stages instead of one jump. FlashMark asks a face-authentication model to hold up against attacks nobody put in its training set. MoMo VL takes vision-language models built for English and teaches them Vietnamese. Different problems, same question.

Research interests

  • Domain adaptation and robustness — how vision models behave on distributions they were never trained on, and how to move them there without labels.
  • Multimodal LLMs for low-resource languages — adapting vision-language models built for English to languages with far less data, Vietnamese in particular.

Also worked substantially on presentation-attack and deepfake detection for face authentication, and reinforcement learning for traffic control — both under industry or lab confidentiality, so less of it is public.
