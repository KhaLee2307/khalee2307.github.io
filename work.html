MoMo VL — a Vietnamese vision-language model

A large vision-language model built for tasks MoMo users actually touch: expense management from receipt photos, merchant validation, the MoMo Local Guide, and general text extraction. Open VLMs handle English well and Vietnamese poorly, so the work was as much about data as about modelling.

  • Led a team of 2 on crawling and filtering Vietnamese web corpora — CafeF, VnExpress, SachVui, CuuDuongThanCong — into a usable training set.
  • Fine-tuned open models (Qwen2.5-VL, InternVL3) on those Vietnamese datasets.
  • Applied DPO and MPO to push the model's reasoning past what supervised fine-tuning alone produced.

FlashMark — passive liveness detection for eKYC

Face authentication that answers one question from a single selfie: real person, or an attack? FlashMark flashes a sequence of colours on the screen and reads the frames where the face reflects those hues — physical skin reflects light in ways a printed photo, a mask, or an injected video stream cannot fake convincingly.

  • Designed and trained four AI modules covering physical attacks (photo spoofing, 3D masks) and cyber attacks (deepfake, stream injection), including building the dataset from scratch.
  • Led a team of 4 collecting real-user data in the MoMo app and generating adversarial data with deepfake and relighting tools.
  • Contributed to iBeta Level 2 certification for MoMo Face Authentication — the industry benchmark for presentation-attack detection.

StrDA — stratified domain adaptation for scene text recognition

Scene-text models are trained on synthetic images and deployed on real photographs. When that gap is wide, existing unsupervised domain adaptation degrades. StrDA closes it in stages instead of one jump: estimate how far each unlabelled sample sits from the source domain, sort the target data into ordered strata, then self-train through them in sequence.

  • First author — method design, all experiments, and the paper. Accepted early to WACV 2025.
  • Proposed the Harmonic Domain Gap Estimator to measure out-of-distribution and domain-discriminative levels per sample.
  • State-of-the-art on STR benchmarks using no human-annotated target data.
